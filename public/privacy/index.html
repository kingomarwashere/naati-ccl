Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

CCLingo ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. When you practise, we process the audio you record and its transcript so our AI can score your interpretation. We store your scores and progress so you can track improvement over time.

How we use your information

We use your data to provide the service: authenticating your account, generating AI feedback, saving your progress, and processing payments for Pro passes. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We use trusted providers to operate CCLingo, including a payment processor (Stripe) for purchases and AI providers for scoring. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their part of the service.

Data retention & your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. We retain account data for as long as your account is active.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us through the app.
