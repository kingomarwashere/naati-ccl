About the NAATI CCL Vietnamese test

The Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test assesses your ability to interpret dialogue between English and Vietnamese. It consists of two dialogues of around 300 words each, split into segments you interpret one at a time. Passing both dialogues earns you 5 bonus points toward skilled migration visas (subclasses 189, 190 and 491).

Our platform lets you practise Vietnamese CCL dialogues across health, legal, employment, housing, education, banking, immigration and more — the exact domains you'll face in the real test.
