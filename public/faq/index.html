Everything you need to know about the NAATI CCL test and CCL Practice.

The Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test is a NAATI-administered exam that assesses your ability to interpret spoken dialogue between English and another language. Passing awards 5 bonus points toward most Australian skilled migration visas (subclasses 189, 190, 491, etc.).
We currently support Nepali, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Punjabi, Sinhala, Mandarin (Simplified & Traditional), Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Dari, Persian/Farsi, Urdu, Indonesian, Thai, Filipino/Tagalog, Korean, Spanish, and more. New languages are added regularly.
Our AI uses the official NAATI marking criteria to evaluate accuracy, key terminology, and register. It's designed as a study tool, not a guarantee of your real exam result. That said, users who consistently score above 70% in practice tend to perform well on the actual exam.
