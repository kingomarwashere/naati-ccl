About CCLingo

CCLingo is an independent online preparation platform for the NAATI Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test.

Every year, thousands of people sit the NAATI CCL test to earn 5 bonus points toward their Australian permanent residency. Those points are often the difference that gets a skilled migration application over the line — yet realistic, affordable practice material has always been hard to find.

We built CCLingo to fix that. Our platform recreates the real exam experience: segmented interpreting dialogues that alternate between English and your community language, scored 0–5 against the official NAATI marking criteria, with a 29/45 pass mark. You can practise across every domain you'll face on exam day — health, legal, employment, housing, education, banking, immigration and community services.

We currently support 11 languages: Mandarin, Cantonese, Punjabi, Hindi, Nepali, Vietnamese, Arabic, Korean, Tamil, Persian and Thai — with more added regularly. Every dialogue comes with native-quality audio, instant AI feedback, full mock exams and progress tracking.
